# Life is a Rollercoaster — Group Chart Activity

You are provided with the **Life is a Rollercoaster** Excel file, and each group is asked to create specific graphs using the data provided. The groups must decide what data to include in the graph. They are only given the graph name. This will assess whether or not you understand the best practice of how to utilize each graph and what data each graph can best represent.

## Instructions

Explain the chart type assigned to your group by providing:

- Its **definition**
- Its **use case**
- An **example**
- Its **advantages and disadvantages**

You have **15 minutes** in the breakout rooms. You will share your findings with the other groups when we return to the main room.

You can then come back into the big group and share your charts (should be well presented as clear visualizations) and discuss whether or not they pulled out the best data for their given chart.
